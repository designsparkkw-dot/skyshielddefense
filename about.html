🌍   Our Story

One Company.
Total Global Protection.

Sky Shield Defence was founded on a singular conviction: that the world's critical assets deserve a defence partner who addresses every threat — physical, aerial and systemic — under one trusted roof.

Our Mission

Protecting Today.
Securing Tomorrow.

Sky Shield Defence exists to protect the things that matter most — critical infrastructure, sovereign assets, private estates, and human lives. We do this by combining three world-leading service disciplines into a single, seamlessly integrated defence partnership.

We are not a product company. We are a solutions company — one that takes the time to understand your unique threat landscape and engineers bespoke protection that evolves with the risks you face.
