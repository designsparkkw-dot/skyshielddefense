Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

1. Introduction

Sky Shield Defence ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address and telephone number from contact forms
  • Company or organisation name and country
  • Details of your security requirements and enquiries
  • Technical information about your device and browser (analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide requested services
  • Send service information and updates relevant to your interests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All data transmission is encrypted using TLS/SSL technology. We maintain strict internal confidentiality protocols appropriate for a security services organisation.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law (including GDPR where applicable), you have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; object to processing; and data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@skyshielddefence.com
