Our Coating Systems

Engineered Protection Systems

Every coating system is formulated for specific environmental demands — delivering maximum performance where it matters most.

Polyurea Spray Coatings

Ultra-fast cure, 100% solids polyurea systems offering exceptional elongation, tensile strength and chemical resistance. Ideal for tanks, containment, marine and industrial applications. Cures in seconds even at sub-zero temperatures.

Polyurethane Systems

Flexible and rigid polyurethane formulations delivering outstanding impact absorption, thermal insulation and waterproofing. Used for roofing, flooring, pipelines and infrastructure protective membranes.

Epoxy Flooring Systems

Seven distinct flooring system options — from heavy-duty industrial to anti-static, slip-resistant and chemical-resistant floors. Engineered for warehouses, manufacturing plants, food processing and defense facilities.

Defense & Ballistic Coatings

Specialised polyurea systems applied to military vehicles, blast walls and structures to contain fragmentation and spalling from explosive detonations. Proven in operational environments worldwide.

Marine & Offshore Protection

Corrosion-proof coatings engineered for saltwater immersion, UV exposure and mechanical impact. Applied to hulls, decks, offshore platforms and port infrastructure with multi-decade performance.

Spray Foam Insulation

Rhino Foam closed-cell and open-cell spray foam systems for superior thermal and acoustic insulation, air sealing and structural reinforcement across commercial and industrial buildings.
