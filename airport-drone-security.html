The Threat

Why Airports Need Anti-Drone Systems Now

Airport drone incursions have become one of the fastest-growing aviation security risks globally. A single unauthorised UAV within a protected airspace zone can force runway closures affecting hundreds of flights, ground entire aircraft fleets and cost an airport millions in operational losses — all within minutes of detection.

Beyond operational disruption, drones represent active security threats: surveillance of airside infrastructure and perimeters, contraband delivery to restricted areas, photography of sensitive facilities, and increasingly — weaponisation. The commercial drone market has made these capabilities available to any motivated actor at minimal cost.

Aviation authorities worldwide are now mandating counter-drone capability at international airports. Sky Shield Defense's airport anti-drone systems are engineered to meet this requirement — providing comprehensive airspace protection that integrates seamlessly with existing ATM and security infrastructure.

Runway Incursion

A drone in the runway environment forces immediate closure and aircraft diversion — affecting all inbound and outbound flights until the airspace is cleared.

Mid-Air Collision Risk

Aircraft on approach at 250+ knots have seconds to react to a drone strike — engine ingestion or windscreen impact can be catastrophic.

Perimeter Surveillance

Hostile actors use drones to map airside infrastructure, security camera positions and patrol patterns — intelligence gathering for future attacks.

Contraband Delivery

Drones bypass physical perimeter security to deliver contraband, communications devices and prohibited items into restricted airside zones.

Common Questions

Airport Drone Security FAQs

What is an airport anti-drone system?
An integrated counter-UAV platform that detects, tracks and neutralises unauthorised drones in airport airspace — without disrupting legitimate aircraft, ATC or ATM systems. Sky Shield Defense combines radar, RF detection, acoustic sensors, optical cameras and AI fusion to identify and defeat hostile UAVs while authorised aircraft continue operations normally.
How do drones threaten airport operations?
Airport drone incursions have risen 400% in five years. A single UAV near a runway forces closures costing $3M+ per hour in diverted flights and delays. Drones also enable perimeter surveillance by hostile actors, contraband delivery to restricted airside areas, and — increasingly — direct weaponised attack on aircraft and airport infrastructure.
Can airport drone systems work without disrupting air traffic?
Yes — this is a core design requirement. Our AI classification algorithms distinguish hostile UAVs from manned aircraft with high precision, and RF defeat is targeted and directional — not broad-spectrum jamming. It does not affect aircraft avionics, ILS or ATC communications. Integration with ATM and UTM systems ensures full airspace coordination throughout.
What regulations govern airport drone security systems?
Systems must comply with national aviation authority regulations, spectrum licensing and ICAO guidelines on UAV operations near aerodromes. Sky Shield Defense provides full regulatory compliance support — spectrum licensing, aviation authority coordination and all compliance documentation — as part of every airport deployment.
How quickly can an airport drone security system be deployed?
Fixed installations typically require 4–12 weeks from contract award depending on airport size and regulatory approvals. Our AEROSENTRY PATROL mobile system provides emergency coverage within 60 minutes for airports that need immediate protection while a permanent system is being procured.
Which airports has Sky Shield Defense protected?
Sky Shield Defense has deployed airport drone security systems across international airports, regional airports and military airfields across MENA and Africa.
