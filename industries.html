Airports & Aviation

Counter-drone airspace protection · Surface coatings · Security governance

Airports face a dual protection challenge: maintaining the structural integrity of vast infrastructure assets while securing airspace from the growing threat of unauthorised drone incursions.

A single drone incident can ground an entire airport — costing millions per hour in lost revenue, triggering safety inquiries and damaging reputations. Sky Shield Detect's AEROSENTRY and AERODOME systems provide comprehensive detection-to-defeat coverage across all runway approaches and terminal airspace.

Our Sky Shield Protect coatings maintain runway surfaces, terminal floors, jet bridges and infrastructure against the extreme wear of aviation operations — extending asset life and reducing costly maintenance downtime.
